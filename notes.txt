



Two main data structures: 
    - Metadata region (page_array)
        - tracks state of all physical pages in system
        - maintains metadata about alloc && free pages
        - each element of page_array represents a physical page start from 0

    - Two lists that track free pages of 4kb and 2MB sizes
    - we maintain the list pointers next and prev inside page_array elements

Two main operations: 
    -split() -> convert 2MB pages into 4KB pages 
    -merge() -> merge 4kb pages into a 4MB page
